**The NSGDP Data Portal is a public catalogue of health datasets, plus a contributor upload path and a staff review pipeline.** Datasets arrive as spreadsheets, go through an automated ingestion engine, get reviewed by two tiers of staff, and then get published.

## Who uses the portal

| Role | Who they are | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Anyone, no login | Browse and download published datasets, maps, and analytics |
| Registered | Signed-in user | Same as public, plus saved items and notifications |
| Contributor | Staff at a development partner | Upload datasets and submit them for review |
| Dev Partner Admin | Lead at a development partner | Manage their own organisation's users and datasets |
| Staff | NSPHCDA staff with permission groups | Validate, approve, publish, and manage content, each limited to their granted capabilities |
| Super Admin | Platform owner | Everything, including granting permissions and dataset hard delete |
